How to Use Blur & Pixelate Image

Follow these simple steps to complete your task — right inside your browser.

1

Upload a photo or screenshot

Drop any JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, BMP, or SVG image up to 20 MB into the dropzone, or paste directly with Ctrl+V.

2

Pick Brush Area or Whole Image

Keep Brush Area selected to censor only painted regions like faces and numbers, or switch to Whole Image to blur or pixelate the entire frame.

3

Choose Pixelate Mosaic or Smooth Blur

Toggle between blocky Pixelate Mosaic tiles or Smooth Blur depending on the style you want.

4

Set strength and Brush Size

Adjust Mosaic Block Size (4–60 px) or Blur Radius (2–50 px), and tune Brush Size (2%–20%) when painting selectively.

5

Paint over sensitive details

Click and drag directly across faces, license plates, or private text on the preview canvas; click Undo or Reset Brush anytime.

6

Choose format and download

Pick Orig, JPG, PNG, or WebP output, set Quality (10%–100%) for lossy formats, and click Download Image to save locally.

How Blur & Pixelate Image Works

You upload a photo and choose blur or pixelate, then either paint the region with a brush or apply the effect to the whole image. The blur radius (18 by default) controls how soft the blur is; the pixel size controls how chunky the pixelation is. The tool composites the effect onto the image on canvas and downloads the result.

Blurring and pixelating destroy information — that is the point. A heavily blurred face cannot be unblurred. Nothing is uploaded; the effect runs on your device.

Examples

Face in a photo you want to share

A bystander’s face in a street photo is pixelated with the brush, so the photo can be posted without identifying them.

Number plate in a car photo

A car’s number plate is blurred before the photo goes on a listing site, so the plate is not public.

Whole image as a background

A photo is blurred across the whole frame to become a soft background behind text on a poster.

Supported Formats

Input: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF, GIF, BMP, SVG and AVIF, up to 20 MB. Effect: blur (radius adjustable, default 18) or pixelate (pixel size adjustable). Scope: brush-painted region (brush size adjustable) or the whole image.

The effect is applied on canvas. The original file is never modified — the download is a new file.

Accuracy & Limitations

A light blur can sometimes be partially reversed by sharpening, so use a strong blur or pixelation for anything sensitive. Pixelation at a small pixel size may still leave text readable — test it before sharing. The brush paints freehand; precise edges need a steady hand and a small brush.
