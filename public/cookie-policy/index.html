Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 Nov 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store on your device. They can remember information about your visit — for example, whether you have seen a message before — so the site or a service on it can recognise your browser on a later visit. This policy explains which cookies are used on CompressMyPic, why they are used, and how you can control them.

2. Cookies We Do Not Set

CompressMyPic itself does not set any cookies. Our image tools run entirely in your browser: compression needs no account, no login, and no cookie. We do not set a cookie to remember you, track you, or store your preferences. Your images never leave your browser, and nothing about them is stored in a cookie.

3. Google AdSense Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. AdSense and its partners may set cookies on your device to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet, and to measure how often an ad is shown. Ad placements on this site are chosen manually; we do not use automatic ad insertion. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com) or https://www.aboutads.info/choices/. Choosing non-personalized ads does not stop advertising altogether, but it means ads are no longer based on your browsing history.

4. Google Analytics 4 Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used — for example, which tools are popular and which pages receive visits. GA4 may set cookies on your device, such as _ga, to distinguish one visit from another. The data is anonymized and used only in summary form; we do not use GA4 to identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

5. Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google shows you a consent message before personalized advertising is shown, in line with the GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the "Privacy and cookie settings" link in the footer of this site, which re-opens the consent message, or by clearing your browser cookies. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything done lawfully before the withdrawal.

6. Other Third-Party Services

A few other services on this site may use cookies or similar storage of their own. Google reCAPTCHA, which protects our contact form from spam, may set cookies to tell humans apart from bots; its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Cloudflare, which hosts this site, may set a security cookie to protect the site from abuse. The anonymous comments under our tools ask only for a name, which is stored by the comments service — not in a cookie on this site.

7. Browser Storage That Is Not a Cookie

Your browser also stores a small amount of information locally that is not a cookie. If you post a comment, your browser may remember the name you typed so you do not have to type it again, and if you switch the site's language, your browser may remember that choice for your next visit. This information stays on your own device, is never sent to our servers, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

8. How to Control Cookies

You can delete cookies already stored on your device and block cookies from being set in the future through your browser settings — every major browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) offers this under its privacy or security settings. Blocking all cookies will not break our image tools, which do not depend on cookies, but it may affect features of third-party services such as ads and analytics. You can also use your browser's private or incognito mode, which deletes cookies when you close the window.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the site changes — for example, if we add a new advertising partner or analytics service. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at bilalayyah@gmail.com or read our Privacy Policy.
