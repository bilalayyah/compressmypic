100KB Image Quality: JPG vs PNG vs WebP – Kaun Sa Best?

100KB par JPG, WebP aur PNG, teeno alag dikhte hain. Ek hi photo ko teeno format me banakar dekhne par farak saaf samajh aata hai.

Is article me aap seekhenge ki har format 100KB par kaise behave karta hai, aur kaun sa format kis kaam ke liye best hai.

Aakhir me aapke paas ek quick reference hoga, jisse aap har baar sahi format turant chun payenge.

Kaise har format kaise kaam karta hai?

JPG photo ko chhota karne ke liye woh detail hatata hai jo aankh ko dikhti nahi. Rang ke halke badlav aur bareek noise pehle hataye jate hain.

WebP bhi wahi karta hai, lekin uska tareeka thoda behtar hai. Isi liye WebP aksar JPG se thodi chhoti file banata hai.

PNG kuch nahi hatata, woh har pixel ko bilkul waisa hi bachata hai. Isi liye PNG photo ke liye bahut badi file ban jati hai.

JPG par 100KB

JPG par chehre wali photo 100KB me bahut saaf rehti hai. Aankh, baal aur kapdon ke kinare, sab kuch theek dikhta hai.

Rang bhi natural lagte hain, kyunki JPG soft rangon ko acchi tarah sambhalta hai. Halke rangon ke beech farak dikhta rehta hai.

Isi liye photo ke liye JPG sabse safe choice hai. Har portal aur har device use khol leta hai.

WebP par 100KB

WebP par wahi photo JPG se thodi zyada saaf dikh sakti hai, kyunki WebP ko kam data me zyada detail bachani aati hai.

Edges bhi WebP me zyada saaf rehte hain, khaas taur par text aur logo ke aas paas. Isi liye WebP blog aur website ke liye accha hai.

Lekin kuch purane portal aur app WebP accept nahi karte. Upload karne se pehle portal ke rules zaroor padh lein.

PNG par 100KB

PNG par photo ko 100KB me sama pana bahut mushkil hai. PNG har pixel ko bachata hai, isliye photo ki file badi rehti hai.

Agar photo PNG me 100KB me sama bhi jaye to uske dimensions bahut chhote hone padte hain. Chhoti photo par detail dikhti hi nahi.

Isi liye photo ke liye PNG kabhi na chunein. PNG sirf text, logo aur transparency ke liye hai.

Photos ki seedhi tulna

Ek hi chehre ki photo ko teeno format me 100KB par banayein. JPG aur WebP me chehra saaf dikhega, PNG me photo chhoti aur dhundhli lagegi.

Rangon me bhi farak hai. JPG aur WebP me rang natural lagte hain, PNG me rang theek hote hain lekin file badi hoti hai.

Aakhir me JPG aur WebP ke beech faisla karna hai. Portal purana ho to JPG, website ke liye WebP behtar hai.

Graphics tulna

Graphics, jaise logo aur icon, me saaf rang aur seedhe kinare hote hain. Isme PNG sabse accha hai, kyunki woh kinare bilkul saaf rakhta hai.

JPG graphics par halke dhabbe dikha sakta hai, khaas taur par saaf rang ke aas paas. 100KB par ye dhabbe chhote hote hain, lekin dikhte hain.

WebP graphics me bhi accha hai, aur file PNG se chhoti banti hai. Website ke logo ke liye WebP ek accha beech ka rasta hai.

Screenshots tulna

Screenshot me text aur saaf kinare hote hain, isliye PNG sabse saaf natija deta hai. Akshar bilkul saaf padhe jate hain.

JPG screenshot par text ke aas paas halke dhabbe bana sakta hai. 100KB par ye dhabbe zyada dikhte hain, kyunki quality kam hoti hai.

WebP screenshot ke liye bhi accha hai, lekin text ki saafai me PNG se thoda peeche rehta hai. Tutorial ke screenshot ke liye PNG chunein.

Transparency ki seedhi tulna

Transparency ka matlab hai photo ka peeche ka hissa dikhna. Sirf PNG aur WebP me transparency hoti hai, JPG me nahi.

Logo ko kisi bhi background par lagana ho to PNG sabse safe hai. Har jagah PNG khul jata hai aur transparency sahi rehti hai.

WebP me bhi transparency hoti hai aur file chhoti banti hai. Lekin purane portal WebP ko support nahi karte, isliye pehle check karein.

Kaun sa format Kab?

Photo ke liye JPG chunein, ye sabse safe aur har jagah chalne wala format hai. Portal ho ya website, JPG har jagah khul jata hai.

Website aur blog ke liye WebP chunein, kyunki file chhoti banti hai aur page jaldi khulta hai. Bas browser support check kar lein.

Text, logo aur transparency ke liye PNG chunein. Photo ke liye PNG kabhi na chunein, woh bahut badi file banata hai.

Test karna method

Sahi format chunne ka sabse accha tareeka hai khud test karna. Ek hi photo ko teeno format me 100KB par banayein.

Teeno ko side by side khol kar dekhein. Chehre, text aur kinare par dhyan dein, farak khud dikh jayega.

Jo format aapki photo par sabse saaf lage, wahi chunein. Har photo alag hoti hai, isliye ek hi format har photo par best nahi hota.

Colour depth

Colour depth ka matlab hai kitne rang photo me dikhaye ja sakte hain. JPG aur WebP me rangon ki koi kami nahi hoti, dono lakhon rang dikhate hain.

PNG me bhi rangon ki kami nahi hoti, lekin woh har rang ko bilkul bachata hai. Isi liye uski file badi hoti hai.

100KB par rangon ka farak kam dikhta hai, kyunki teeno format me rang kaafi hote hain. Farak sirf detail aur file size me dikhta hai.

Sharpness vs smoothness

Sharpness ka matlab hai kinare kitne saaf dikhte hain. PNG me kinare sabse saaf hote hain, kyunki woh kuch nahi hatata.

JPG me kinare halke soft ho jate hain, lekin chehre aur rang ke liye ye softness acchi lagti hai. Photo natural dikhti hai.

WebP beech me hai, kinare JPG se saaf aur PNG se thode soft. Website ke liye ye balance sabse accha hai.

Format galat fehmiyan

Ek galat fehmi ye hai ki PNG hamesha best hota hai, kyunki woh quality nahi khota. Photo ke liye ye galat hai, PNG bahut badi file banata hai.

Dusri galat fehmi ye hai ki WebP har jagah chalta hai. Aaj bhi kuch purane portal aur app WebP accept nahi karte.

Teesri galat fehmi ye hai ki JPG purana hai, isliye bekar hai. Photo ke liye JPG aaj bhi sabse safe aur behtareen choice hai.

100KB files ko archive kaise karein

Har format ki file ko alag folder me rakhein, jaise JPG, WebP aur PNG. Aise naam se baad me dhoondhna asan hota hai.

Original file ko hamesha safe rakhein. Usse aap baad me kisi bhi format me file bana sakte hain.

File ke naam me format aur size likhein, jaise photo-100kb.jpg. Aise naam se ek nazar me sab kuch pata chal jata hai.

Quick reference

Photo ke liye JPG chunein, ye sabse safe hai. Har portal aur har device par chal jata hai.

Website ke liye WebP chunein, file chhoti banti hai aur page jaldi khulta hai.

Text, logo aur transparency ke liye PNG chunein. Photo ke liye PNG kabhi na chunein.

Lossy vs lossless: asal farak kya hai?

Lossy ka matlab hai kuch data hatakar file chhoti banana. JPG aur WebP lossy hain, isliye woh photo ko chhota kar sakte hain.

Lossless ka matlab hai kuch bhi hataye bina file bachana. PNG lossless hai, isliye uski file badi rehti hai.

Photo ke liye lossy behtar hai, kyunki hataya gaya data aankh ko dikhta nahi. Text ke liye lossless behtar hai, kyunki har pixel matter karta hai.

Browser support: kaun sa format kahan chalta hai

JPG har browser me chalta hai, chahe woh kitna bhi purana ho. Isi liye portal aur form me JPG sabse safe hai.

WebP aaj ke sabhi naye browser me chalta hai, lekin bahut purane browser me nahi. Website ke liye WebP theek hai, portal ke liye pehle check karein.

PNG bhi har browser me chalta hai. Isi liye logo aur transparency ke liye PNG sabse safe choice hai.

Aane wala time formats

AVIF ek naya format hai jo WebP se bhi chhoti file banata hai. Lekin abhi har portal use accept nahi karta.

Aane wale waqt me AVIF aur WebP zyada chalenge, lekin JPG bhi kahin nahi jayega. Portal hamesha JPG ko support karte rahenge.

Isliye aaj ke liye JPG seekhna kaafi hai. Naye format aane par unhe bhi try kar sakte hain.

Kaise quality hai picked?

Tool quality ko khud dhoondhta hai, aapko number chunne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Woh binary search se sabse acchi quality nikalta hai jo 100KB me sama jaye.

Har format ke liye quality alag hoti hai. JPG me 75 percent quality, WebP me 70 percent par bhi wahi natija mil sakta hai.

Isi liye format badalne par file ka size aur quality, dono badal jate hain. Khud test karke dekhein ki aapki photo par kaun sa format best hai.

JPG sabse safe hai, har portal aur device par chal jata hai. WebP thoda chhota banta hai, lekin purane portal use accept nahi karte. Photo ke liye PNG na chunein.

Kya WebP JPG se hamesha behtar hota hai?

File size ke hisab se haan, WebP aksar thoda chhota banta hai. Lekin portal aur purane app me WebP nahi chalta, isliye wahan JPG hi behtar hai.

PNG photo 100KB me kyun nahi sama pati?

PNG har pixel ko bilkul bachata hai, kuch nahi hatata. Isi liye photo ki file bahut badi rehti hai. Photo ke liye JPG ya WebP chunein.

Screenshot ke liye kaun sa format best hai?

PNG, kyunki usme text aur kinare bilkul saaf rehte hain. JPG screenshot par text ke aas paas halke dhabbe bana sakta hai.

Kya main JPG ko WebP me badal sakta hoon?

Haan. Format converter tool 25 MB tak ki file leta hai aur kaam browser me hi karta hai. JPG se WebP banane ke baad file thodi chhoti ho jati hai.

Transparency ke liye kaun sa format chunein?

PNG sabse safe hai, har jagah chal jata hai. WebP me bhi transparency hoti hai aur file chhoti banti hai, lekin purane portal use support nahi karte.
