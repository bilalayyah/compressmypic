Blur karne se pehle photo ko ek folder me rakh lein aur original ka backup alag jagah save kar lein, taake dhaka hissa wapas laya ja sake.

Shuru Karne Se Pehle: Kya Chahiye

Blur karne ke liye photo aur browser ke alawa kuch nahi chahiye. Tool online chalta hai, isliye Android ya iPhone par bhi kaam karta hai.

Blur karne se pehle ye tay kar lein ke photo ka kaunsa hissa chhupana hai. Chehra, number ya background — har ek ke liye alag setting hoti hai.

Blur karne se pehle original photo ka backup le lein. Dhaka hua hissa wapas nahi aata, isliye original ko safe rakhein.

Blur Karne Ke Steps

Tool kholein, photo upload karein, blur ya pixelate chunein, brush se hissa dhakein, taakat set karein, preview dekhein aur download kar lein. Bas, itna hi kaam hai.

Blur tool me brush, taakat aur download — sab kuch ek hi screen par milta hai.

Steps ko ek baar me samajh lein, phir photo par try karein. Pehli baar me hi sahi result milega.

Blur Ya Pixelate: Kaunsa Chunein

Chehra ya text chhupana ho to pixelate chunein, kyunki wo zyada safe hai. Background soft karna ho to blur chunein, kyunki wo zyada natural lagta hai.

Dono ka kaam ek hi hai, par dikhne me farq hai. Pixelate me bade tukde dikhte hain, blur me dhundhlapan dikhta hai.

Kaam ke hisab se chunein. Sensitive cheez ke liye pixelate, style ke liye blur.

Brush Mode Kaise Use Karein

Brush mode me photo ke hisse par brush se rang karein. Jahan brush lagega, wahi hissa blur ya pixelate hoga.

Brush chalate waqt photo ko zoom karke dekhein — zoom par hi pata chalta hai ke brush sirf wahi hissa dhak raha hai jo chhupana hai, baaki photo saaf reh rahi hai.

Brush ko dheere move karein. Jaldi me brush galat jagah lag sakta hai aur photo kharab ho sakti hai.

Poori Photo Blur Karne Ka Mode

Whole image mode me poori photo blur ya pixelate ho jati hai. Ye tab use karein jab poori photo hi sensitive ho.

Whole image mode on karne ke baad taakat set karein. Halki taakat se photo ka andaza rehta hai, gehri taakat se detail khatam ho jati hai.

Poori photo blur karne se pehle ek baar soch lein ke kya poori photo hi chhupani hai — aksar sirf ek chehra ya ek number chhupana kaafi hota hai, baaki photo saaf rehni chahiye.

Brush Size Kaise Set Karein

Brush ki size us hisse ke hisab se chunein jo dhakna hai — chehre jaisi badi jagah ke liye bada brush, jab ke chhote number ya text ke liye chhota brush zyada theek rehta hai.

Brush ki size slider se asani se badli ja sakti hai — size set karte waqt photo ko zoom karke dekhein, taake brush bilkul us jagah par ho jahan use hona chahiye.

Bade brush se kaam jaldi hota hai, lekin kinare khurdare reh jate hain — chhota brush dheere chalta hai, par us se dhake hue hisse ke kinare zyada saaf aur barabar bante hain.

Blur Ki Taakat Kaise Set Karein

Taakat slider se set ki jati hai. Halka blur sirf soft karta hai, gehra blur detail ko poori tarah hata deta hai.

Chehra chhupane ke liye blur gehna hona chahiye, taake pehchan bilkul na ho — jab ke background ko sirf halka soft karna ho to halka blur hi kaafi hota hai.

Blur ki taakat set karte waqt preview par nazar rakhein — agar dhake hue hisse ki detail ab bhi dikh rahi ho to taakat badhate jayein, jab tak woh detail poori tarah gayab na ho jaye.

Galti Ho Jaye To Kaise Theek Karein

Galat hissa blur ho jaye to undo button se wapas jayein. Har step ko undo kiya ja sakta hai.

Agar bahut zyada blur ho gaya ho to taakat kam karke dobara try karein. Hamesha original se kaam karein.

Galti hone par ghabrayein nahi. Undo se har galti theek ho jati hai, aur original backup me safe rehta hai.

Preview Dekhkar Compare Kaise Karein

Download se pehle preview me blur wali aur bina blur wali photo ko compare karein. Farq saaf dikhna chahiye.

Preview me photo ko zoom karke dekhein ke chehra ya text pehchana to nahi ja raha. Agar pehchana ja raha ho to taakat badayein.

Blur karne ke baad preview dekhne me do minute lagte hain, par ye check batata hai ke sensitive hissa poori tarah dhaka ya nahi.

Export Format Kaise Chunein

Blur ki hui photo ko usi format me save karein — JPG JPG hi rahe, PNG PNG hi rahe. Format wahi rahe to quality bachi rehti hai.

Blur karne ke baad agar photo me transparent background hai to PNG chunein, warna transparency khatam ho jayegi.

Blur ki hui photo ko save karne se pehle tay karein ke wo kahan use hogi. Social media par JPG, document ke liye PNG — har kaam ka format alag hota hai.

Phone Ya Tablet Par Blur Karna

Tool mobile browser par bhi chalta hai. Photo upload karein, brush se dhakein aur download kar lein.

Mobile par ek do photos aasani se blur ho jati hain. Bade kaam ke liye desktop behtar hai, kyunki brush ko control karna desktop par asaan hota hai.

Mobile par blur karte waqt photo ko zoom karke dekhein, taake brush sahi jagah lage.

Screenshot Blur Karna

Screenshot me naam, email ya password chhupane ke liye brush mode use karein. Poora text dhakna zaroori hai, ek bhi akshar na dikhe.

Screenshot blur karne ke baad preview me zoom karke check karein ke koi akshar padha to nahi ja raha.

Screenshot ko blur karte waqt baaki hissa saaf rakhein, taake screenshot ka matlab samajh me aaye.

Document Blur Karna

Document me naam, number ya address chhupane ke liye brush mode use karein. Poora hissa dhakna zaroori hai.

Document blur karne ke baad use PDF me badal kar save kar lein, taake record saaf rahe.

Document ko blur karte waqt baaki text ko saaf rakhein, taake document ka matlab samajh me aaye.

Problem Ka Hal

Brush kaam na kare to page ko refresh karke dobara try karein. Kabhi kabhi browser me glitch aa jata hai.

Blur ke baad bhi chehra pehchana ja raha ho to taakat badayein ya pixelate try karein. Pixelate zyada safe hai.

Blur ki hui photo download na ho to file ka size check karein aur dobara try karein. Download adhoora hone par file kharab nikalti hai.

Blur Karne Ke Quick Tips

Pehle ek photo par blur try karein, phir baaki karein. Ek photo par taakat sahi ho to baaki sab par wahi taakat lagayein.

Chehra ya text chhupate waqt gehra blur ya pixelate use karein. Halka blur safe nahi hota.

Blur karne se pehle original ka backup le lein, taake kuch gadbad ho to wapas ja sakein.

Presentation Ke Liye Blur Karna

Presentation ke liye blur ko halka rakhein, taake slide saaf dikhe. Bahut gehra blur slide ko kharab kar deta hai.

Presentation me blur karte waqt text ko saaf rakhein, taake slide padhi ja sake.

Tool kholein, photo upload karein, brush mode chunein aur chehre par brush karein. Poora chehra dhakna zaroori hai, aankh aur munh dono. Chehre ke liye pixelate zyada safe hai, kyunki blur me chehra kabhi kabhi halka sa pehchana ja sakta hai.

Blur ya pixelate — kaunsa behtar hai?

Chehra ya text chhupana ho to pixelate, kyunki wo zyada safe hai. Background soft karna ho to blur, kyunki wo zyada natural lagta hai. Kaam ke hisab se chunein.

Blur lagane se pehle kya dhyaan rakhein taake photo kharab na ho?

Nahi, agar file save ho gayi ho to. Blur aur pixelate dono hi detail ko hata dete hain, aur wapas nahi la sakte. Isliye original ka backup rakhein.

Brush mode me text par brush karein. Poora text dhakna zaroori hai, ek bhi akshar na dikhe. Preview me zoom karke check karein ke koi akshar padha to nahi ja raha. Pixelate is kaam ke liye zyada safe hai.

Phone par photo blur karne ke liye kya karna hota hai?

Blur kitna gehra hona chahiye?

Itna gehra ke detail poori tarah khatam ho jaye. Chehra ya text pehchana na ja sake. Preview me zoom karke check karein — agar detail dikh rahi ho to taakat badayein.
