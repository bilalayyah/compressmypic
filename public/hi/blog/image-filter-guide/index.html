Ek click me photo ka mood badal jaye — Vintage se purani yaadein, Noir se drama, Warm se dhoop. Photo filter yahi karta hai.

Is guide me samjhenge ke filter kya hain, 11 styles me se kaunsa kab use karein, aur ek jaisa look kaise banayein.

Filter lagana asaan hai, par sahi filter chunna ek skill hai. Dono seekhenge.

Filter Asal Me Kya Karte Hain

Filter photo ke rang, roshni aur contrast ko ek saath badal deta hai. Ek click me photo ka poora mood badal jata hai.

Filter photo ko purana, garam, thanda ya kaala-safed bana sakta hai. Har filter ka apna mood hota hai.

Filter photo ke pixels ko badalta hai, par original ko nahi mitata. Filter hataya bhi ja sakta hai, agar original backup me ho.

11 Filter Styles: Ek Nazar Me

Tool me 11 filter styles hain — Vintage, Sepia, B&W Noir, Warm, Cool aur 6 aur. Har style ka apna rang aur mood hai.

Style chunne se pehle photo ka mood dekhein. Purani yaadon ke liye Vintage, drama ke liye Noir, dhoop ke liye Warm.

11 styles me se har photo ke liye koi na koi sahi milta hai. Ek ek karke try karein aur jo pasand aaye, wahi chunein.

Filter Intensity: Halka Ya Gehra

Intensity slider se filter ko halka ya gehra kiya ja sakta hai. Halka filter sirf mood badalta hai, gehra filter photo ko poori tarah badal deta hai.

Intensity ko halka rakhein, taake photo natural lage. Zyada intensity photo ko nakli bana deti hai.

Intensity set karte waqt preview me dekhein. Filter saaf dikhe, par photo bhi saaf rahe.

Vintage Default Kyun Hai

Tool Vintage filter se shuru hota hai, kyunki ye sabse zyada pasand kiya jane wala style hai. Vintage purani aur yaadon wali feeling deta hai.

Vintage pasand na aaye to koi aur style chun lein. Default sirf shuruaat hai, aapko wahi rakhna zaroori nahi hai.

Vintage ka halka version zyada acha lagta hai. Intensity ko kam karke Vintage ko soft kiya ja sakta hai.

Grayscale Vs Noir: Farq Samjhen

Grayscale photo ko seedha kaala-safed bana deta hai, bina kisi drama ke. Noir kaala-safed ke saath gehra contrast bhi deta hai.

Grayscale soft aur purana lagta hai, Noir bold aur dramatic lagta hai. Dono ka mood alag hai.

Portrait ke liye Noir behtareen hai, kyunki wo chehre ko sharp dikhata hai. Grayscale landscape ke liye acha hai.

Sepia, Vintage, Matte Aur Golden

Sepia photo ko bhoora-purana rang deta hai, jaise purani tasveer. Vintage bhi purana lagta hai, par usme rang halke hote hain.

Matte photo ko soft aur halka bana deta hai, jaise film ki photo. Golden photo ko garam aur sunahra rang deta hai.

Chaaro style purane zamane ka mood dete hain, par har ek ka rang alag hai. Photo ke hisab se chunein.

Warm Vs Cool: Garam Ya Thanda

Warm filter photo ko garam rang deta hai — peela aur narangi. Dhoop aur shaam ki photo ke liye Warm behtareen hai.

Cool filter photo ko thanda rang deta hai — neela aur halka. Subah aur barf ki photo ke liye Cool behtareen hai.

Warm aur Cool ka farq samajhne se photo ka mood sahi banta hai. Photo ka waqt aur jagah dekh kar chunein.

Invert Filter Kya Karta Hai

Invert filter photo ke rangon ko ulta kar deta hai — safed kaala, kaala safed. Photo bilkul alag dikhti hai.

Invert artistic aur ajeeb look ke liye use hota hai. Aam photo ke liye ye sahi nahi hai.

Invert try karne se pehle original ka backup le lein, taake wapas ja sakein.

Original Dekhne Ke Liye Hold Karein

Hold karke original photo ko dekha ja sakta hai. Button ko dabakar rakhein to bina filter wali photo dikhti hai, chhod dein to filter wali.

Hold se farq saaf dikhta hai. Filter sahi hai ya zyada ho gaya — hold karke pata chal jata hai.

Hold ko har filter ke baad use karein, taake photo kharab na ho.

Side By Side Compare Karna

Side by side me pehle wali aur baad wali photo ko saath me dekha ja sakta hai. Farq saaf dikhta hai.

Compare karne se pata chalta hai ke filter sahi hai ya zyada ho gaya hai.

Compare mode ko har filter ke baad use karein, taake photo kharab na ho.

Portrait Photo Ke Liye Filter

Portrait ke liye Warm ya Vintage behtareen hai, kyunki wo chehre ko naram aur acha dikhate hain.

Portrait me filter ko halka rakhein. Zyada filter chehre ko kharab kar deta hai.

Portrait me filter lagane ke baad skin tone ko check karein. Skin ka rang natural lagna chahiye.

Landscape Photo Ke Liye Filter

Landscape ke liye Warm ya Cool behtareen hai. Warm dhoop ke liye, Cool subah aur barf ke liye.

Landscape me filter ko halka badayein, taake rang natural lagein. Zyada filter se ghaas neon lagti hai.

Landscape me filter lagane ke baad horizon ko check karein. Rang saaf aur natural dikhne chahiye.

Ek Jaisa Look Kaise Banayein

Ek jaisa look banane ke liye har photo par ek hi filter aur ek hi intensity lagayein. Is se feed ya album saaf lagti hai.

Consistent look ke liye filter ki setting ko likh lein — kaunsa filter, kitni intensity. Har baar wahi use karein.

Ek jaisa look brand banata hai. Log aapki photos ko pehchanne lagte hain.

Format Aur Export

Filter lagane ke baad photo ko JPG me export karna behtareen hai, kyunki file chhoti hoti hai aur har jagah chalti hai.

Transparency wali photo ke liye PNG use karein, taake transparency bachi rahe.

Format chunne se pehle ye tay karein ke photo kahan use hogi. Har platform ka apna format hota hai.

Privacy: Aapki Photos Aapke Device Par Rehti Hain

Tool aapke browser me chalta hai, isliye aapki photos server par upload nahi hoti. Filter lagana aapke device par hi hota hai.

Iska matlab hai ke personal photos par bhi bina kisi risk ke filter lagaya ja sakta hai. Koi bhi aapki photo ko dekh nahi sakta.

Filter lagane ke baad browser tab band kar dein, taake aapki photo ka koi nishan na rahe.

Filter Lagate Waqt Aam Galtiyaan

Sabse badi galti hai filter ko bahut gehra lagana. Gehra filter photo ko nakli bana deta hai aur detail khatam kar deta hai.

Doosri galti hai har photo par alag filter lagana. Consistency na hone se feed ya album kharab lagti hai.

Teesri galti hai original delete kar dena. Filter wali file ko alag naam se save karein aur original ko backup me rakhein.

Travel Photo Ke Liye Filter

Travel photo ke liye Warm ya Vintage behtareen hai, kyunki wo jagah ki yaadon ko zinda karte hain.

Travel photo me filter ko halka rakhein, taake rang natural lagein. Zyada filter se jagah nakli lagti hai.

Travel photo me filter lagane ke baad rangon ko check karein. Rang zinda lagein, par asli bhi lagein.

Filter photo ka mood hai — sahi style chunein, intensity halki rakhein. Abhi try karne ke liye Image Filter tool kholein, steps ke liye Photo par filter kaise lagaye padhein, aur tulna ke liye Photo filter tulna dekhein.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Portrait ke liye sabse acha filter kaunsa hai?

Warm ya Vintage. Dono chehre ko naram aur acha dikhate hain. Filter ko halka rakhein, taake skin tone natural lage. Noir bhi portrait ke liye acha hai, kyunki wo chehre ko sharp dikhata hai.

Filter intensity control kya karta hai?

Ye filter ko halka ya gehra karta hai. Halka filter sirf mood badalta hai, gehra filter photo ko poori tarah badal deta hai. Intensity ko halka rakhein, taake photo natural lage.

Tool Vintage filter se kyun shuru hota hai?

Kyunki Vintage sabse zyada pasand kiya jane wala style hai. Ye purani aur yaadon wali feeling deta hai. Vintage pasand na aaye to koi aur style chun lein — default sirf shuruaat hai.

Kya ek photo par do filter laga sakta hoon?

Ek waqt me ek hi filter lagta hai. Doosra filter lagane se pehla hat jata hai. Alag look chahiye ho to photo ko ek filter ke saath save karein, phir use dobara kholkar dusra filter lagayein.

Kya filter se photo ki resolution badalti hai?

Nahi. Filter sirf rang aur roshni badalta hai, pixels ki ginti nahi. Photo ki resolution wahi rehti hai. Filter lagane ke baad file ka size thoda badal sakta hai, par resolution nahi badalti.

Kya apni personal photo par filter lagana browser tool me safe hai?

Haan. Tool aapke browser me chalta hai, isliye aapki photo server par upload nahi hoti. Filter lagana aapke device par hi hota hai aur koi bhi aapki photo ko dekh nahi sakta.
