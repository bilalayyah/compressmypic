Kai Images Ek Saath Resize Kaise Kare – Step by Step

Kai images ko ek saath resize karna seekhein: batch upload karein, mode chunein aur download karein. Sab kuch 2 minute me.

Kai photos ko ek saath resize karna bahut aasan hai. Is guide me aap 6 steps me seekhenge ki batch kaise taiyaar kare, strategy kaise chune aur ZIP ko kaise download kare.

Hum ek blog ke 60 photos wale folder se chalenge, jinhe 1200 pixels ki width par lana hai. Wahi folder har step me example banega.

Aakhir me aap product shoot, blog folder ya social set ko ek baar me resize kar sakenge, bina har photo ko alag se khole.

6 Steps Me Batch Resize Kare

  1. Bulk Image Resizer Tool khole aur apni photos ko upload kare.
  2. Batch ko taiyaar kare aur strategy chune.
  3. Preset ya size type kare aur quality aur format set kare.
  4. 2-3 photos ka preview check kare.
  5. ZIP download kare aur extract kare.
  6. Photos ko check karke use kare.

Blog folder ki 60 photos ko in 6 steps me resize kiya gaya. Upload karte hi saari photos list me aa gayi, phir Uniform Width 1200 set karte hi preview update ho gaya.

Poora batch 3 minute me taiyaar ho gaya. Har photo ko alag se resize karne me ghante lag jate.

Pehle Apna Batch Taiyaar Kare

Batch taiyaar karne ka matlab hai photos ko ek folder me ikattha karna aur unhe check karna. Kharab ya duplicate photos ko pehle hata de.

Blog folder me 60 photos thi, lekin 5 duplicate nikli. Unhe hatane ke baad 55 photos bachi, jo saari resize ki gayi.

Photos ko naam ke hisaab se arrange kare, taaki download ke baad unhe pehchan na aasan ho. Original ka backup bhi rakhe.

Batch Ke Liye Strategy Chune

Strategy chune: Max Fit, Uniform Width, Exact ya Percentage. Har strategy ka apna kaam hai.

Blog folder ke liye Uniform Width 1200 chuna gaya, kyunki blog me saari photos ek column me dikhti hain aur unki width barabar honi chahiye.

Strategy badalne par preview turant update ho jata hai. Alag strategies ko try karke dekhe ki kaun si aapke batch par best lagti hai.

Preset Lagaye Ya Size Type Kare

Preset ready-made size hai, jaise blog 1200 ya Instagram 1080. Ek click me sahi size lag jata hai.

Blog folder ke liye blog preset 1200 lagaya gaya. Isse width aur height apne aap set ho gaye.

Agar preset se thoda alag size chahiye to use haath se type kare. Preset ke baad bhi size ko adjust kiya ja sakta hai.

Quality Aur Format Set Kare

Quality ko 80 se 90 percent par rakhe, taaki photos sharp dikhe aur file bhi chhoti ho. Blog photos ke liye 85 percent best hai.

Format chune: photo ke liye JPG, aur transparency ke liye PNG. Blog folder ki photos ke liye JPG chuna gaya.

Quality aur format set karne ke baad preview check kare. Low quality par photo blurry ho jati hai.

Download Se Pehle Results Check Kare

Preview me 2-3 photos ko dhyaan se dekhe. Size, quality aur ratio sab theek hone chahiye.

Blog folder ke preview me pehli 3 photos check ki gayi. Saari 1200 pixels ki width par thi aur sharp dikh rahi thi.

Preview me teesri photo ka ratio galat laga to strategy ko Max Fit se Uniform Width par badla gaya, phir dobara check karke hi download kiya gaya.

ZIP Download Kare

Saari resized photos ko ek ZIP file me download kare. Isse saari photos ek saath mil jati hain.

Blog folder ki 55 photos ZIP me download ki gayi. ZIP ko extract karne ke baad saari photos naam ke hisaab se mili.

ZIP ko blog wale folder me extract kiya gaya aur 55 photos ko naam ke hisaab se check kiya gaya, taaki koi photo missing na ho.

Batch Me Mixed Orientations

Blog folder me portrait wali 12 aur landscape wali 43 photos thi, aur Uniform Width se dono ki width 1200 ho gayi bina ratio bigde.

Blog folder me kuch portrait aur kuch landscape photos thi. Uniform Width lagane se saari ki width 1200 ho gayi, lekin height apne ratio ke hisaab se alag rahi.

Mixed orientations ke liye Exact strategy avoid kare, kyunki usse kuch photos stretch ya crop ho sakti hain.

Product Shoot Ko Resize Kare

Product shoot ki photos ko ek size me karna zaroori hai, taaki catalogue saaf dikhe. Max Fit 1000 by 1000 iske liye best hai.

Product shoot me 40 photos thi, jinhe Max Fit se resize kiya gaya. Saari photos box ke andar aa gayi aur catalogue professional lagne laga.

Product photos ke liye quality ko 90 percent par rakhe, taaki product sharp dikhe aur customer ko detail samajh aaye.

Blog Folder Ko Resize Kare

Blog folder ki photos ko 1200 pixels ki width par resize kare, taaki blog tez load ho. Isse blog ka layout bhi saaf rehta hai.

Blog folder me 55 photos thi, jinhe Uniform Width 1200 se resize kiya gaya. Blog ab pehle se tez load hota hai.

Blog photos ke liye JPG format aur 85 percent quality best hai. Isse photo sharp bhi rehti hai aur file bhi chhoti hoti hai.

Social Set Ko Resize Kare

Social set me har platform ke liye alag size hota hai. Instagram ke liye 1080, aur Facebook ke liye 1200 best hai.

Social set ko platform ke hisaab se alag alag batch me resize kare. Ek batch me ek platform ka size rakhe.

Social photos ke liye preset use kare, taaki sahi size mil jaye. Galat size par platform photo ko khud crop kar deta hai.

Phone Se Batch Resize Kare

Phone ke browser me tool khole aur gallery se photos upload kare. Chhota batch phone par bhi resize ho jata hai.

Phone par 10 se 20 photos tak theek hai, usse zyada ke liye computer best hai. Bada batch phone par slow ho sakta hai.

Phone par 15 photos ka chhota batch preset se resize kiya gaya, kyunki phone par numbers type karna slow hota hai.

Batch Resize Ki Problems Kaise Suljhaye

Agar koi photo resize na ho to use dobara save karke try kare. Corrupt file process nahi hoti.

Agar kuch photos blurry dikhe to no-upscale protection ko on kare. Chhoti photo ko bada karne se woh blurry ho jati hai.

Agar ZIP download na ho to browser ko dobara try kare ya photos ko chhote batch me baat le.

Repeat Batches Aur Workflow

Agar aapko har hafte naya batch resize karna hai to wahi settings ko note karke rakhe. Isse har baar dobara set nahi karna padta.

Blog ke liye har hafte nayi photos aati hain. Wahi Uniform Width 1200 ki setting har baar use ki jati hai, taaki blog consistent rahe.

Workflow ko simple rakhe: photos ko folder me rakhe, batch upload kare, settings lagaye aur ZIP download kare.

Quick Checklist: Batch Resize Karne Se Pehle

Batch taiyaar kare, strategy chune aur preset ya size set kare.

Quality aur format set kare, 2-3 photos ka preview check kare aur ZIP me download kare.

ZIP download karne ke baad original 60 photos wala folder alag se sambhal kar rakha gaya, taaki dobara resize karna ho to original mile.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Bada batch resize karne me kitna time lagta hai?

Batch ke size par depend karta hai. 50 photos ka batch 2-3 minute me ho jata hai, aur 100 photos ka 5 minute me. Phone par bada batch thoda slow ho sakta hai.

Agar batch me ek image fail ho jaye to kya hoga?

Baaki photos phir bhi resize ho jayengi. Fail hui photo ko dobara save karke alag se resize kare, ya use batch se hatakar dobara try kare.

Kya main batch complete hone ke baad aur photos add kar sakta hu?

Nahi, batch complete hone ke baad nayi photos add nahi hoti. Nayi photos ke liye naya batch banana hoga, ya pehle se saari photos ko ek saath upload kare.

Batch resize karne ki koi limit hai?

Koi fixed limit nahi hai, lekin bahut bada batch browser ko slow kar sakta hai. 50 se 100 photos ek baar me best hai, usse zyada ko 2-3 hisson me baat le.

Kya resized photos ke naam original jaise hi rehte hain?

Haan. ZIP me photos ke naam original jaise hi rehte hain, isliye unhe pehchan na aasan hota hai aur upload karte waqt confusion nahi hoti.

Pehli baar batch resize karne ke liye best size kaun sa hai?

10 se 20 photos se shuru kare. Isse aapko settings samajh aayengi aur koi galti ho to use theek karna aasan hoga. Phir bada batch kare.
